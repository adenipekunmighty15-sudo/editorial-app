Rooted is a digital sanctuary and community platform dedicated to Christian literature, spiritual formation, and honest storytelling. Designed to help users "read deeply and live fully," it serves as an ecosystem where faith and literature intersect. The core experience centers on a Curated Library of Christian fiction and non-fiction, where users can track reading progress and save recommended titles. To combat digital isolation, Rooted features community-driven Book Clubs, focusing on small, intimate groups of five people to ensure rich, deep conversations. The Story Room provides a creative hub for the community to publish personal essays and reflections, supported by a dedicated writing interface. The app distinguishes itself through immersive technology, utilizing Three.js and GSAP to create a cinematic 3D leather-bound book preview that brings a tactile feel to the digital page. Integration of autonomous AI further enhances the experience via the Writer's Assistant, which analyzes story drafts to suggest genres and help authors organize their narrative structure. Visually, the platform employs a serene palette of terracotta and sage to evoke peace, complemented by daily biblical invitations and a habit-tracking streak system. By blending a quiet, intentional design with modern AI and 3D graphics, Rooted transforms reading from a passive activity into a communal, spiritual journey. It is more than a tool; it is a thoughtfully crafted environment for those seeking spiritual growth, honest expression, and meaningful connection within a supportive Christian community.
